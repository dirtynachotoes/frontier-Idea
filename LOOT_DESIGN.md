# Loot design — deferred, not implemented

Do not add a content library until real bidirectional gameplay and persistence work. The first loop should have one deterministic discovery, one Guardian capability unlock, one real combat objective and one NMS technology reward. Neither side may be optional.

Later loot should vary by actions/capabilities, archetype/frame, element, curated perks and source identity. Bosses get recognizable small pools; rare discoveries can offer known rewards; repeatable activities can mix curated chase drops and useful resources. Record discovery ownership and repeated drops before applying duplicate weighting or bad-luck mitigation. Build-defining drops need a verified native implementation and a clear benefit/tradeoff, not just increased damage.

A future item definition must identify the native engine/catalog/version, recipe/instance ID, origin activity, campaign/sandbox provenance, allowed profile, unlock conditions and observable effect. The core owns reward intent and reconciliation; the destination engine owns actual item semantics. Additive grants require idempotent durable intent IDs and native reconciliation, not the current counter protocol.

No rarity tables, exotic effects, weapon rolls, perk pool, loot director or item grants are supplied in this probe. Parhelion is a research dependency for supported equipment authoring, not a promise that every described exotic is implementable.
